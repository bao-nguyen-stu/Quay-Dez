# Quay-Dez

## Project Overview

In Vietnamese culture, music is the heartbeat of every milestone, from birthdays and graduations to family gatherings. However, digital greetings often feel static and impersonal, while professional music video making is too complex for everyday partygoers. 

Inspired by Vietnamese quẩy culture, Quẩy Đêz is a web-based, interactive celebration platform designed to bridge that gap, transforming any browser into an accessible, high-energy party stage for friends and family.

## Technical Exploration

### Audio-Reactive Generative Visuals

Real-time frequency band analysis (bass, kick, mids, highs via Web Audio API) driving Canvas 2D kaleidoscopes, morphing mandalas, neon stripes, particles, and UI elements.

### Patch-Based Motion Tracking

Frame-by-frame patch template matching with smoothing and tilt control to dynamically lock face photos onto moving characters in uploaded GIFs or videos.

### Live DJ Performance Engine

Interactive performance controls (decks, pads, keys 1–5) and a mirrored second-screen layout designed for real-time live audience sets.

## Target Audiences

- Students and Young Adults: Friends celebrating birthdays, graduations, and milestones.

- Families and Kids: Relatives of all ages, including young children who can easily play and tap along thanks to the intuitive, accessible controls.

- Party Enthusiasts: Anyone wanting a fun, browser-based way to mix music and visuals without needing professional VJ software.

## Future Improvements

Looking ahead, future improvements for Quẩy Đêz include refining the UI with a distinct visual identity, enhancing face-tracking accuracy, and adding video exports for sharing sets. Further enhancements could feature lyric-based AI music generation, interactive digital surprise cards, transparent GIF support, 3D animated characters and objects, true GIF face-swapping instead of simple image overlays, new real-time VJ effects, and fresh internet trend integrations.
